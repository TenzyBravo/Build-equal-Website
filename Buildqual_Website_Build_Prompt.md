# Website Build Prompt — Buildqual Engineering Limited

## 1. Project Summary

Build a professional, modern, mobile-responsive **business website** for **Buildqual Engineering Limited**, a specialist civil engineering materials and geotechnical testing company based in Lusaka, Zambia. The site's job is to establish credibility, showcase the full range of testing services, display completed projects, and generate qualified enquiries from contractors, consulting engineers, and government/infrastructure clients across Zambia.

**Built by:** Double ZZ Company (Tech & Creative Agency)
**Target audience:** Civil engineering contractors, consulting engineers, road authorities, mining/energy firms, construction project managers.
**Primary goals:** (1) Look trustworthy and technically competent, (2) Explain services clearly, (3) Drive contact/quote enquiries.

---

## 2. Brand & Design Direction

- **Tone:** Professional, industrial, precise, trustworthy. This is an engineering firm — the design should feel solid and credible, not flashy.
- **Color palette:** Engineering/industrial theme.
  - Primary: deep navy / steel blue (e.g. `#0a2540`, `#12395c`)
  - Accent: safety orange / amber (e.g. `#ff7a00`) for buttons and highlights
  - Neutrals: white, light grey (`#f4f7fb`), charcoal text (`#1a2332`)
- **Typography:** Clean, legible sans-serif (e.g. Inter, Roboto, or Montserrat for headings). Strong headings, comfortable body text.
- **Imagery:** Photos of field/lab testing, road construction, soil/concrete/aggregate sampling, PPE-wearing technicians, site equipment. Use placeholders where real photos aren't yet available, and label them clearly for the client to swap in.
- **Feel:** Grid-based, spacious, with subtle shadows and rounded corners. Include trust signals (accreditations, standards references, client logos, project stats).

## 4. Company Content (use verbatim or lightly polished)

### About Us
Buildqual Engineering Limited is a specialist testing company serving the civil engineering and geotechnical sector. It offers a full range of testing services for soils, aggregates, bitumen, asphalt, and concrete, plus geotechnical investigations — for new roads, borrow pits, road rehabilitation, gravel roads, township developments, buildings, and structures. Buildqual has operated field laboratories on projects countrywide across Zambia.

### Vision
To be the leading service provider in our field of operation and to create long-lasting relationships with our clients through value-adding products and services.

### Mission
To provide accurate test results on time and to improve continually.

### Core Values
- **Excellence** — Always providing quality testing for our customers.
- **Respect** — Taking time to understand and value each person and respecting their choices.
- **Integrity** — Acting with honesty and openness, and doing what we say we will do.
- **Innovation** — Striving to be industry leaders.

### Why Choose Us
- Professional and friendly service.
- Accurate testing that meets or exceeds client expectations.
- Qualified, dedicated team with years of industry experience.
- Superior customer service and competitive prices.
- Quality work delivered on time.

### Service Categories (with descriptions)

**Soils & Gravels**
A wide range of tests on soils and gravels to give engineers and contractors accurate results for determining the characteristics and quality of construction materials before, during, and after construction. Tests can be carried out on site by competent staff as required.

**Concrete & Precast Products**
A wide range of concrete tests done in the laboratory. Site services can be arranged for sampling and testing such as making concrete cubes and coring of concrete. Concrete mix designs and mix verifications can also be performed.

**Aggregate Testing (Hard Rock Aggregate)**
Aggregate testing to help clients assess the characteristics and quality of aggregates for use in concrete, building/construction work, or asphalt mixes. Conducted by highly qualified staff.

**Geotechnical Investigations**
A wide range of geotechnical investigations delivered by skilled professionals with years of industry experience, including:
- New Road Investigations
- Structural Development Investigations
- Township / School Sanitation Investigations
- Water Pipeline Investigations
- Reservoir / Pump Station Investigations

Field capabilities include: Geological mapping, Dynamic Cone Penetrometer (DCP), Rotary Drilling / SPT, Soil Profiling, Compaction Testing, Concrete Cube Testing, Schmidt Hammer Test, Core Logging, California Bearing Ratio (CBR), Flakiness Test, Atterberg Limit Test, and Asphalt Core Drilling.

### Safety, Health & Environment
Buildqual Engineering Limited is committed to providing safe and healthful working conditions and safe work practices that protect employees and others at all work locations. No task is so important that it cannot be done safely. The company places great emphasis on caring for the environment and aims to minimize the use of harmful products, using environmentally friendly products and procedures wherever possible.

## 9. Technical Requirements

- **Recommended stack:** Choose one appropriate to the developer/tool:
  - Static/JAMstack: HTML + Tailwind CSS + a lightweight JS framework, or Next.js/Astro.
  - CMS option: WordPress with a clean custom theme (good if the client wants to self-manage content easily).
- **Hosting:** Deployable to standard hosting; SSL (HTTPS) required. Domain suggestions: `buildqual.co.zm` or `buildqualengineering.com`.
- **Forms:** Server-side or form service (e.g. Formspree/EmailJS) if static.
- **Code quality:** Clean, commented, well-structured; reusable components; no console errors.
- **Cross-browser:** Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge.
- **Deliverables:** Full source code, deployment instructions, a short content-editing guide for staff, and basic on-page SEO configured.
