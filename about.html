Our Story

Who we are

Buildqual Engineering Limited is a specialist testing company serving the civil engineering and geotechnical sector. We offer a full range of testing services for soils, aggregates, bitumen, asphalt, and concrete, plus geotechnical investigations — for new roads, borrow pits, road rehabilitation, gravel roads, township developments, buildings, and structures.

Buildqual has operated field laboratories on projects countrywide across Zambia, delivering accurate results that engineers and contractors rely on.
