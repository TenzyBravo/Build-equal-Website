The California Bearing Ratio (CBR) test is one of the most widely used strength indicators for road subgrades and sub-bases. It gives engineers a straightforward comparison between the resistance of a compacted material and a standard crushed-stone reference.

## Why it matters

Zambia's road network is expanding rapidly, and pavement performance depends heavily on the quality of the layers beneath the surface. A subgrade with poor CBR values will settle unevenly, crack the surface, and shorten the road's design life.

## How Buildqual runs the test

We follow TMH 1 A8 (natural) and A9 (stabilized) methods. Samples can be prepared on site or brought to our laboratory, cured to the specified moisture content, and tested under a standard penetration load.
