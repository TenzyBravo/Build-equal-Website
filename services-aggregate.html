Aggregate Testing (Hard Rock)

Assess the characteristics and quality of aggregates for concrete, construction, and asphalt mixes.

Overview

What we test

Aggregate testing to help clients assess the characteristics and quality of aggregates for use in concrete, building/construction work, or asphalt mixes. Conducted by highly qualified staff.

Key tests offered

  • • Aggregate crushing value (ACV)
  • • 10% fine aggregate crushing value (FACT)
  • • Flakiness index
  • • Sieve analysis (coarse & fine)
  • • Bulk density of coarse and fine aggregates
  • • Relative density
  • • Marshall stability & flow (briquettes)
  • • Asphalt bulk relative density
  • • Indirect tensile strength (asphalt)
  • • Maximum theoretical relative density (Rice's method)
  • • Binder content

On-site testing

Sampling from stockpiles, binders, asphalt, slurry mix and treated layers.

Laboratory testing

Full grading, density, strength, and asphalt suite.
