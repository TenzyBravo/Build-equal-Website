Concrete & Precast Testing

Cube testing, coring, slump tests, mix designs and mix verifications — for reliable concrete outcomes.

Overview

What we test

A wide range of concrete tests done in the laboratory. Site services can be arranged for sampling and testing such as making concrete cubes and coring of concrete. Concrete mix designs and mix verifications can also be performed.

Key tests offered

  • • Manufacturing, curing and compressive strength of concrete cubes (TMH 1 D1)
  • • Slump test (TMH 1 D3)
  • • Coring from concrete layers (TMH 5 MC2)
  • • Concrete mix designs
  • • Concrete mix verifications
  • • Sampling of fresh concrete (TMH 5 MB series)

On-site testing

Site sampling, cube making, and coring services on request.

Laboratory testing

Curing, crushing, and strength reporting for compliance and QA.
